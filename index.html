The plan

One wedding. Three boats.
Five days at sea.

Instead of a single day, we’re taking the people we love most on a voyage. On the afternoon of Wednesday 21 July 2027, a private flotilla of three crewed boats will leave Naples together and spend five days wandering the islands of the Gulf of Naples and the Amalfi Coast: swimming in coves, eating fresh seafood and handmade pasta together on deck, and dropping anchor each night somewhere beautiful.

Somewhere in that week, we’ll get married. The details of the day are still coming together, and we’ll share more as we get closer to the event.

The crews do the sailing and the cooking. Your only job is to get yourself to Naples and be on holiday with us.

The voyage

Day by day

  1. Wednesday, July 21

    Naples → Ischia

    We board at Mergellina harbour in Naples in the afternoon, then set sail for the island of Ischia with swim stops along the way. First night moored at Sant’Angelo, Ischia’s prettiest fishing village.

  2. Thursday, July 22

    Around Ischia

    A slow lap of the island to Lacco Ameno, with swimming and snorkelling stops all day, a long lunch, and aperitivo on deck. Second night on Ischia.

  3. Friday, July 23

    Procida → Capri → Positano

    Morning in the painted cove of Corricella on Procida, then on to Capri to sail past the Faraglioni rocks, with the option of a few hours ashore. We anchor for the night off Positano. After dinner, walk the town if you like.

  4. Saturday, July 24

    Along the coast to Amalfi

    A lazy morning swim in a quiet bay on the tip of the Sorrentine peninsula, then a slow sail along the coast to Amalfi for the last night in the bay.

  5. Sunday, July 25

    Salerno & farewell

    A short morning run to Marina d’Arechi in Salerno, where we disembark mid-morning, hug, and start planning the reunion.

Life aboard

What the week looks like

Every table set together

All meals for the whole group are served at the flagship’s long deck table: fresh seafood and handmade pasta cooked aboard by the chef, with the catamarans rafted alongside.

Wine with every sunset

Local wine, beer and prosecco are included all trip: Falanghina and rosé in the coolers, aperitivo on deck as standard.

A private cabin

Every couple gets a private cabin behind a door that closes, aboard an air-conditioned boat.

Full crews

Every boat is professionally crewed, with a full crew of four on the flagship. Nobody is asking you to hoist anything.

Swim stops & shore time

Cliffside towns, hidden coves, snorkelling off the stern, and optional evenings ashore in Capri and Positano.

The toy locker

Paddleboards, snorkel gear, tenders for shore runs, and a floating pool that gets anchored off the back at swim stops.

Not included: flights and airport transfers. Once you’ve booked, send us your flight details and we’ll help coordinate arrivals closer to the date.

Getting there

The logistics, in one glance

  1. Jul 20–21

    Fly into Naples International (NAP). Some of us are coming in the night before to make a pizza night of it; others will arrive the morning of the 21st and meet us in the city. Either works, and a hotel the night before is entirely optional.

  2. Jul 21

    Board at Mergellina

    We board at Mergellina harbour in the afternoon. Exact boarding details and your boat assignment will come with your invitation.

  3. Jul 25

    Disembark in Salerno

    The voyage ends Sunday mid-morning at Marina d’Arechi, Salerno, about an hour from Naples by train or car, with good onward connections. Book flights home for the afternoon or evening, or stay on and keep the holiday going.

Book early: Naples hosts the America’s Cup in summer 2027, so flights and hotels that week will fill up faster and cost more than a normal July. The moment you know you’re coming, lock in the flight, and the hotel too if you’re staying over.
